# Starship Ring — Habitat & Protective Structure

## Status

**CANON:** District 14 is an artificial habitat in space protected by a massive ring structure. The ring is a foundational part of the world concept.

**OPEN:** The precise ring geometry, dimensions, materials, orientation, structural layout, relation to the inhabited space, and protective mechanisms are not yet decided. “Starship Ring” is the working name/reference from the concept discussion; it must not be treated as a fully engineered design.

## Role in the setting

The ring establishes the boundary between inhabited District 14 and outer space. It is not merely a decorative skyline object. It is envisioned as a major habitat structure that may carry or visually communicate protection, environmental containment, infrastructure, maintenance access, thermal and power systems, and illumination systems. Those functions are fictional design possibilities, not validated engineering claims.

The ring should be monumental and industrial in character. Avoid making the habitat look like a thin glass bubble or a transparent dome unless that is chosen in a future design decision.

## Visual storytelling

From within the streets, the ring should be revealed selectively:
- distant arcs or segments that help communicate immense scale;
- structural ribs, service bands, lights, or maintenance zones where appropriate;
- occasional views toward the ring interior or its infrastructure;
- a silhouette that remains legible but does not dominate every street view;
- atmospheric depth that allows ring features to fade naturally into distance.

Visibility should be designed for street-level perspectives, rooftops, aerial travel, and any high vantage point. A player should gradually understand the enclosure rather than see a full explanatory diagram at every moment.

## Design constraints

- Ring scale must be derived from the three-street masterplan, not chosen independently.
- It must accommodate the approved city footprint and height envelope.
- It must not visually or physically intersect buildings, aerial routes, or player-accessible rooftops unless an intersection is intentionally designed.
- Avoid repeating ring segment patterns so close together that the structure reads as a small game prop.
- Use controlled lighting. The ring should feel infrastructural, not like a decorative neon hoop.
- Preserve a clear exterior-space backdrop where views are intended.

## Decisions still required

1. Is the ring circular, elliptical, elongated, multi-arc, or another geometry?
2. Does it surround the habitat in plan, in section, or both?
3. What are the visible structural and service layers?
4. How does it relate to artificial gravity or other orientation rules, if any?
5. What produces and maintains the atmosphere?
6. What is the lighting system and day/night cycle?
7. Where are access, maintenance, docking, and emergency systems located?
8. What can the player see from Main Street, rooftops, and aerial corridors?
