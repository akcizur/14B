# Three Streets — Urban Structure

## Status

**CANON:** District 14 contains exactly three streets. Main Street is the dominant long street. The world is designed around a controlled, linear urban spine with limited secondary street connections.

**OPEN:** Exact street topology, relative lengths, orientation, intersections, and end conditions require a masterplan.

## Street hierarchy

### Street 01 — Main Street

The primary urban spine. It should be long, continuous in perception, and lined by residential and mixed-use building rows on both sides. The view should lead toward a distant horizon, atmospheric vanishing point, or designed terminal landmark without exposing an obvious abrupt map boundary.

Main Street supports the most recognizable street life and navigation. Its character may vary gradually along its length: entrances, shopfronts, service areas, stops, small plazas, parking pockets, vegetation, and distinct building clusters. Variations should not destroy its continuous identity.

### Street 02 — Secondary Street

A subordinate street that supports local access, spatial variation, and connectivity. Its scale, length, and function should be clearly lower in hierarchy than Main Street. It must not create the impression of a broad, repeating grid.

### Street 03 — Secondary Street

The other subordinate street. It can differ in character and connection from Street 02, but remains part of the same compact three-street system. The exact distinction between these two streets remains open until a map is designed.

## Design principles

- Street count is fixed at three; do not procedurally add more streets.
- Main Street has the strongest continuous sightline and street frontage.
- Secondary streets should not rival Main Street in length or prominence.
- Avoid a conventional city grid and repetitive parallel avenues.
- Streets may have different profiles and local character.
- Connections must be intentional, readable, and documented.
- Technical world-looping or streaming logic must not appear as a circular road to the player.
- World expansion should prioritize vertical space and longitudinal Main Street development, not horizontal sprawl.

## Street-segment design

The existing 36 m segment is a prototype length only, not an approved universal module. Before repeating it, validate:
- façade continuity and realistic block rhythm;
- pedestrian crossings and access;
- building entry spacing;
- vehicles, parking, loading and service access;
- street furniture and tree placement;
- vertical infrastructure and aerial corridor clearance;
- sightlines and occlusion;
- streaming seams and technical loop behavior.

## Required masterplan deliverable

Create a top-down plan with:
- ring/habitat boundary;
- all three street centerlines and street footprints;
- intersection and connection points;
- Main Street length and visible sightline;
- street frontage and building zones;
- vertical/air-route overlays;
- access and service areas;
- player start and major landmarks.

Until the plan is reviewed, exact street positions and topology remain OPEN.
