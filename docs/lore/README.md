# District 14 — World Lore

This folder is the living reference for the setting, its fictional history, physical rules, mood, and visual identity. It is separate from implementation documentation: lore describes **what the world is**, while engineering documents describe **how the game builds and runs it**.

## Canon status

- **Established:** The habitat exists in space, protected by a massive ring structure; it contains exactly three streets; one is the long, dominant Main Street; the world uses an artificial atmosphere and develops vertically.
- **Working direction:** Czechoslovak urban and residential architecture, primarily 1950–1975, combined with restrained retro-futurist/cyberpunk technology.
- **Undecided:** The ring's final geometry, scale, construction details, precise street placement and connectivity, artificial-atmosphere mechanism, lighting source, chronology, population, and transport rules.
- **Proposals are not canon:** Current masterplan recommendations must be reviewed and approved. Prototypes are not canon.

## Documentation rule

Every lasting worldbuilding decision, accepted correction, important rejected option, open question, or lore-relevant implementation discovery must be recorded here or linked from the relevant technical document. Do not silently turn a prototype value into canon. Keep uncertainty visible.
