# Lore Change Log

This log records important setting decisions and revisions. Implementation commits have their own Git history and verification log.

## 2026-11-21 — Space habitat and three-street premise

- **Added / CANON:** District 14 is an artificial urban habitat located in outer space.
- **Added / CANON:** The habitat is protected by a massive ring structure; “Starship Ring” is the current working name/reference.
- **Added / CANON:** An artificial atmosphere exists within the protected environment.
- **Added / CANON:** The city has exactly three streets.
- **Added / CANON:** Main Street is the long dominant street; two streets are secondary.
- **Added / CANON:** The city may grow vertically through buildings, roofs, elevated infrastructure, and aerial transport.
- **Added / WORKING DIRECTION:** Czechoslovak architecture and urban character from 1950–1975, with restrained retro-futurist/cyberpunk technology.
- **OPEN:** ring shape, dimensions and mechanism; artificial atmosphere and lighting mechanism; exact street map; chronology and society; traffic rules; detailed palette.
- **Documentation action:** established the lore folder as the central index and separated canon, provisional design, and open questions.

## 2026-11-21 — Masterplan Concept 01
- **Added / PROPOSAL:** an elongated capsule/stadium-shaped habitat enclosure as the leading shape study, to support the long linear Main Street without implying a circular road.
- **Added / PROPOSAL:** Street 02 and Street 03 as shorter, staggered cross-street branches from Main Street, rather than parallel streets or a grid. Topology remains awaiting approval.
- **Added:** pre-implementation design gates for habitat plan/section, street topology, Main Street cross-section, building prototype, vertical mobility, atmosphere/lighting, and expansion.
- **Clarified:** “Starship Ring” is a working concept/reference and does not establish real spacecraft engineering or resolved fictional physics.
- **Clarified:** previous air-lane heights and 36 m segment length are prototype values only, not approved canon.
- **Rule:** record proposals separately from canon; do not build additional geometry until the masterplan has been reviewed.
